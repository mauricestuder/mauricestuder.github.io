Questions

Frequently asked.

What does the website cost each year?

Only your own address (domain) with hosting, about 15 francs a year, directly with Infomaniak in your name. I earn nothing from it, and you're not tied to me.

How long does it take?

The site is usually done in one week at most. All I need is your details and quick feedback on the draft.

And if something changes later?

I update new opening hours or prices for you — by the hour or with yearly upkeep.

Do I need a cookie notice?

No. My sites use no cookies, no tracking and no embedded Google maps. So no notice is needed.

You're 15 — is this serious?

Look at the examples and decide for yourself. The draft is free: you see the result before you pay anything.
