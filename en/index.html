Questions

Frequently asked.

What does the website cost each year?

Only your own address (domain) with hosting, about 11 francs a year, directly with Infomaniak in your name. I earn nothing from it, and you're not tied to me.

How long does it take?

You usually get the first draft within a week. How quickly the site goes live after that depends on how fast the details and feedback come in.

And if something changes later?

I update new opening hours or prices for you — by the hour or with yearly upkeep.

No. My sites use no cookies, no tracking and no embedded Google maps. So no notice is needed.
